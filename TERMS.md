# Terms of Service (Demo Draft)

**Status: prototype/demo document, not legally reviewed.** This is a template
suitable as a starting point for a real pilot deployment's legal review, not
a production-ready legal document.

## 1. Overview

SahakarSetu is a cooperative-owned digital platform connecting customers,
institutions, and Labour Cooperative Societies for household and community
services, operated in the context of Smart India Hackathon problem statement
SIH26089 (Ministry of Cooperation / National Council for Cooperative
Training).

## 2. Accounts

- Users register as a Customer, Worker, Cooperative Society Admin, or
  Institution, and must provide accurate information.
- Workers are onboarded through, and remain accountable to, their Labour
  Cooperative Society. Verification status is determined by the cooperative
  society and/or platform administrators.

## 3. Bookings & Payments

- Prices shown are estimates; final price is confirmed on service
  completion.
- The Fair Wage split (worker payout / welfare contribution / cooperative
  operations) is configured by each cooperative society and shown
  transparently before and after every booking.
- Payments in this prototype are processed through a demo payment provider
  and do not involve real financial transactions unless explicitly
  integrated with a licensed payment gateway in production.

## 4. Cancellations & Disputes

- Bookings may be cancelled per the in-app cancellation policy prior to
  service start.
- Disputes are handled through the in-app grievance system and escalated to
  the relevant cooperative society or platform administrator.

## 5. Worker Conduct & Verification

- Workers are expected to act professionally and safely at all times.
- False certification claims or misrepresentation of identity may result in
  suspension pending cooperative society review.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

This is prototype software built for a hackathon demonstration. It is
provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. A real deployment would
require formal legal review, insurance arrangements, and regulatory
compliance specific to the jurisdiction of operation.

## 7. Governing Law

To be determined by the operating cooperative federation and legal counsel
prior to any real-world pilot.

# Worker Cooperative Membership Agreement (Demo Draft)

**Status: prototype/demo document, not a binding legal agreement.**

## Purpose

This agreement template describes the relationship between a worker and
their Labour Cooperative Society when using SahakarSetu, consistent with a
cooperative-ownership model (as opposed to a conventional gig-platform
employment-classification model).

## 1. Cooperative Membership

The worker is, and remains, a member of their Labour Cooperative Society.
SahakarSetu is a digital tool used by the cooperative society to discover
and coordinate work opportunities for its members — it is not the worker's
employer.

## 2. Fair Wage Commitment

- The cooperative society sets the welfare contribution and cooperative
  operations percentages applied to each booking; these are visible to the
  worker on every completed job.
- The worker's payout percentage is never less than what the cooperative
  society's configured split allows, and is shown transparently before
  acceptance is required for any job (fixed pricing is shown at booking
  time).

## 3. Verification & Conduct

- The worker consents to identity, skill, and certification verification by
  their cooperative society.
- The worker agrees to represent their skills and certifications honestly.
- The cooperative society may suspend a worker's account for verified
  misconduct, following its own internal grievance process.

## 4. Welfare & Insurance

- Workers enrolled in the cooperative welfare scheme are entitled to the
  benefits described in their Welfare Profile (visible in-app under
  Welfare).
- Claims are submitted in-app and reviewed by the cooperative society.

## 5. Data & Privacy

See `PRIVACY.md` for what data is collected and how it is used.

## 6. Termination

A worker may leave their cooperative society and stop using the platform at
any time; a cooperative society may remove a member consistent with its own
bylaws and this platform's conduct standards.

---

*A real deployment should have this document reviewed and formally adopted
by each participating Labour Cooperative Federation's legal counsel, and
should reflect the specific cooperative bylaws and any applicable labour
regulations.*
